# Point of view

Settled is a calm motion personality. Entrances decide quickly and then settle slowly, with no overshoot. Exits are shorter than entrances. One element holds the eye at a time while the rest stay still, and the payoff, the figure or message the piece exists to deliver, is held still at the end.

This personality is invented for this example. It is no real brand's, and it has no outside source.
