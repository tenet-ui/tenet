# Feature showcase

A six-second scene at `frame.fps` that shows one feature. It holds a title, one product visual and one proof number, and the eye is led to them one at a time.

1. The title reveals (title reveal) and holds.
2. The product visual enters on `motion.settle` and holds while the proof number is read.
3. The proof number counts up (number counter) and holds still for the last second.

Only the element that is entering moves. Colours and type come from the tokens.

Source: invented for this example; no outside source.
